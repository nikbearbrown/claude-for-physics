As several people have pointed out, a very
strong ring around a spherically symmetricobject is in an unstable gravitational equilibrium,
which means that if it stays perfectly put,it’s fine, but if it gets nudged even just
slightly off its perfectly centered position,it will then get pulled farther and farther
from that position, since the part now closerto the earth gets pulled more strongly toward
the earth than the part now farther from earth.So, it’s just like a perfectly balanced
pencil.
