imprisoned in our solar system forever?Many have claimed this is the case, but a few scientists have actually figured it out.Ever since Enrico Fermi asked his famous question “Where is everybody?”We’ve pondered the mystery of the Fermi
Paradox.In a galaxy where billions of planets have
